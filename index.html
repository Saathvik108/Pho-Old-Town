Our Story

A lifetime of flavor,
brought to your bowl.

"

Born and raised in Hanoi, Vietnam, my passion for cooking began at a very young age. I've always enjoyed preparing delicious meals for my family and friends, cherishing the moments when they savor the dishes I've made with care. For me, it's a way of expressing infinite love. At Pho Old Town, I proudly offer a menu filled with dishes that are close to my heart – from Pho to Bun, Rice dishes, and Banh Mi – all embodying the true flavors of Vietnam. I take great pride in the meticulous preparation process, ensuring every detail is perfect. Our pho broth is simmered for over 24 hours, capturing the essence of many aromatic herbs and delivering a rich, authentic Northern Vietnamese flavor that is sure to transport you back to my homeland. ❤️

— Chef & Founder, Pho Old Town

Our Menu

Dishes close to our heart

Every dish on our menu carries the soul of Vietnam — ingredients sourced with care, prepared with the same love we'd give to family.

Signature

Pho Combination

Our crown jewel. A deeply aromatic Northern Vietnamese pho with slow-simmered 24-hour bone broth, a blend of brisket, flank, tendon & meatballs, topped with fresh herbs and bean sprouts.

Pho

Bun Bo Xao Nam Bo

Stir-fried lemongrass beef over silky rice vermicelli with pickled daikon, shredded lettuce, and a house nuoc cham dressing.

Bun

Banh Mi Combo

Crispy French-Vietnamese baguette loaded with house-made pâté, seasoned meats, pickled vegetables, jalapeños & cilantro.

Banh Mi

Vietnamese Fried Egg Rolls

Golden crispy chả giò filled with seasoned pork, glass noodles, wood ear mushrooms, and carrots. Served with lettuce wraps & nuoc cham.

Appetizer

Vermicelli with Egg Rolls

Cold rice vermicelli topped with crispy egg rolls, fresh herbs, crushed peanuts, and our signature house fish sauce dressing.

Bun

Grass Jelly Desserts

A refreshing chè dessert — silky grass jelly with coconut milk, pandan syrup, and crushed ice. A beloved Vietnamese street food classic.

Dessert

Rice Ball Desserts

Chewy glutinous rice balls with sweet ginger broth base — a warming, comforting dessert with deep roots in Vietnamese tradition.

Dessert

Vietnamese Iced Tea

Refreshing artisan tea served over ice — a cool, lightly sweetened complement to your meal, just as it's enjoyed across Vietnam.

The secret

24 hours in every bowl.

Great pho cannot be rushed. Our chef rises before dawn each day to tend to the broth — bones, charred ginger and onion, star anise, cinnamon, cloves, and more — simmering low and slow for over 24 hours until every bowl carries the full depth of Hanoi in a single sip.
